Complex: Knights Circle
Phase: Phase 2
Date: August 2022
Source: r/ucf
Review: 
Lived there for a year with no terribly bad experiences beyond the internet going out for one whole day twice in the whole year and a bathtub with drainage issues. There were also quite some bugs but i lived first floor and this is Florida.

I didn't really use the amenities so I can't speak for those. My apartment was also in Phase 2 so I had to walk around 20 minutes to get to Alafaya/Publix.

Liked the constant shuttles and the open areas were nice to walk around during the winter. Considered my room and the living room to be decently sized after living in the dorms. It's also a quiet place in comparison to other complexes. Includes all utilities and I never went over the electricity cap despite having the ac at 70-72C almost all the time. It was also on the most affordable end at least back in 2020.

I have heard horror stories from others, tho. Such as water being out for several days or cockroach infestations. So it seems it's mostly just how lucky you are to get into a building with those problems or not.

Most student housing kinda sucks and you'll hear different people with different opinions on the same places (with some exceptions). It is kind of a gamble but i think i liked it better than the place i moved to later
