Complex: Knights Circle
Phase: Phase 2 and Phase 3
Date: June 2022
Source: r/ucf
Review: I lived in Phase 2, the furthest from Alafaya, and Phase 3, the closest to Alafaya, over my 5 years at Knights Circle. I enjoyed Phase 2 since it was quiet. Phase 3 you can hear the traffic depending on which building you are in.

Also for the shuttle, Phase 2 was nice since it was the first stop and the shuttle was generally not full. I had several instances where I had multiple shuttles pass my stop in Phase 3 due to being full. Especially if you are using it at a prime time.

My Phase 3 apartment had mold due to a small leak from the apartment above that wasn't found until my kitchen ceiling caved in with water. That is the only time I can think of where there was mold.
